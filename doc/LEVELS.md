# Tutorial - Job Interview
Scene: Asteroid field
Purpose: Show how weapons and time travel works
Boss: Small, easily defeatable
	--> Starts at Laupeerium bar ~25%
	--> Focused on Dialogs, introduces features in increment
	--> Laser
	--> Chain Lightning
	--> Mine
	--> Rewind (Mine Triggers, also killing the player)
	--> Battle Restart (Some additional drones arrive)
	--> After Drones are eliminated, Boss appears

# Level 1 - Badly guarded Team Pocket Co. Laupeerium Silo Heist
Scene: Team Pocket Co. Silo
Purpose: Gather More Laupeerium
Objective: Destroy Enemy drones, Blast open Silo door
Boss: Big Laser boss
	--> Base is surrounded by asteroids, drones guard it
	--> Eliminate the drones
	--> blast open the Silo doors
	--> Victory! Laupeerium bar fills to 75%

# Level 2 - Infiltration ( stealth campaign? )
Scene: Team Pocket co. Universal Space base
Purpose: Make use of rewind ability
Objective: Destroy center before time runs out
	--> Go to the center of the base, and blast open the Silo doors
	--> Doors revealed to be the core of the base, which destroys the base itself

# Level 3 - Showdown
Scene: The ruins of Team Pocket co. Universal Space base
Purpose: Fight 3 bosses all at once
Objective: Survive
	--> Best way to win is to make the bosses fight each other
	--> Bosses Drop enough Laupeerium for ~100%

# Level 4 - Fight with the Ancients
Scene: Raggedy Planet
Purpose: Introduce the Ancients
Objective: Destroy drones together with the Ancients
	--> Time travel maneouver executed
	--> accident occures, ancient spaceships are pulled from the past
	--> Drones arrive to destroy the planet, player fights alongside Ancients
